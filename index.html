Bilkul 👍 **Life ke about English Speaking Questions** — easy English + easy Urdu: ### 1. What is the most important thing in life? **Answer:** I think family, health, and happiness are the most important things in life. **Urdu:** Mere khayal mein family, health aur happiness zindagi mein sab se important hain. ### 2. What is your biggest goal in life? **Answer:** My biggest goal is to become successful and live a peaceful life. **Urdu:** Mera sab se bara goal successful banna aur pur-sukoon zindagi guzarna hai. ### 3. What have you learned from life? **Answer:** I have learned that nothing is easy, but hard work can make things better. **Urdu:** Maine seekha hai ke kuch bhi asaan nahi hota, lekin mehnat cheezon ko behtar bana sakti hai. ### 4. What makes life beautiful? **Answer:** Love, family, good friends, and beautiful memories make life beautiful. **Urdu:** Mohabbat, family, achay dost aur khoobsurat yaadein zindagi ko khoobsurat banati hain. ### 5. What do you do when life becomes difficult? **Answer:** I stay calm, think positively, and try to find a solution. **Urdu:** Jab zindagi mushkil hoti hai to main pur-sukoon rehta hoon, positive sochta hoon aur solution dhoondta hoon. ### 6. Do you think money can buy happiness? **Answer:** Money can make life easier, but true happiness comes from good relationships and peace of mind. **Urdu:** Paisa zindagi ko asaan bana sakta hai, lekin asli khushi achay relationships aur sukoon se milti hai. ### 7. What is one lesson you will never forget? **Answer:** I will never forget that failure is not the end. It is a chance to learn and improve. **Urdu:** Main kabhi nahi bhoolunga ke failure end nahi hota. Yeh seekhne aur improve karne ka mauqa hota hai. ### 8. What kind of life do you want? **Answer:** I want a simple, happy, successful, and peaceful life. **Urdu:** Main ek simple, khush, successful aur peaceful life chahta hoon. ### 9. If you could change one thing about your past, what would it be? **Answer:** I would not change my past because every experience has taught me something. **Urdu:** Main apna past change nahi karunga kyun ke har experience ne mujhe kuch na kuch sikhaya hai. ### 10. What advice would you give to your younger self? **Answer:** I would say, “Believe in yourself, work hard, and never give up.” **Urdu:** Main kahunga, “Khud par yaqeen rakho, mehnat karo aur kabhi haar mat mano.” ### ⭐ Bonus Speaking Question **Q: If life gives you a second chance, what will you do differently?** **Answer:** I will be more confident, take more opportunities, and spend more time with the people I love. **Urdu:** Main zyada confident rahunga, zyada opportunities loonga aur jin logon se mohabbat karta hoon unke saath zyada waqt guzaroonga.
